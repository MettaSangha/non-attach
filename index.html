A Teaching

The Teaching
of Non-Grasping

Freedom begins when we stop asking reality to become something other than what it is.

Enter the teaching ↓
The First Seeing

Suffering begins when we demand that reality be different from what it is.

Life does not remain still. Everything that appears eventually changes. What arises passes away.

People change. Bodies change. Fortunes change. Thoughts arise and disappear. Even the sense of “me” changes.
◌

The mind suffers when it tries to freeze what cannot be frozen.

The Practice

Non-grasping does not mean withdrawing from life. It means participating fully without clinging.

01

When something pleasant comes, enjoy it without demanding that it remain.

02

When something painful comes, experience it without believing it will last forever.

03

When uncertainty comes, allow uncertainty.

04

When action is required, act.

05

When nothing can be done, let go.

Freedom

Freedom is not getting everything you want.

Freedom is no longer needing reality to obey your preferences.

See clearly.
Do not cling to what changes.
Do not resist what cannot be changed.

See clearly. Act wisely. Let go completely.
That is the Dharma.
