Dharma

The Teaching
of Non-Grasping

Suffering begins when we demand that reality be different from what it is.

Life is always changing.
People change. Bodies change.
Fortunes change. Thoughts arise and disappear.

Even the sense of “me” is constantly changing.

The mind suffers when it tries to freeze what cannot be frozen.

When something pleasant comes, enjoy it without clinging.

When something painful comes, experience it without resistance.

When uncertainty comes, allow uncertainty.

When action is required, act.

When nothing can be done, let go.

Freedom is not getting everything you want.

Freedom is no longer needing reality to obey your preferences.
See clearly.
Act wisely.
Let go completely.
That is the Dharma.
